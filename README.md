# Academic Portfolio

## About Me

* I am an academic professional with 16+ years of experience in academia, with a strong focus on teaching, research, academic administration, and student development.

* Currently, I am working as an Associate Professor in the Department of CSE at SIMATS Engineering.

* My academic experience includes teaching, curriculum development, student project supervision, departmental leadership, workshops, accreditation activities, and industry collaboration. I have also served as a Department Head for 7 years, contributing to academic planning, departmental operations, and institutional activities.

* Before entering full-time academia, I worked for 8 years as a Senior Software Engineer, gaining professional experience in software development, system architecture, enterprise applications, and team collaboration.

* My academic interests are complemented by this industry experience, enabling me to connect academic concepts with practical applications.

## Selected Research Work

Software Transactional Memory & Concurrency Control

Research on concurrency control mechanisms for multi-threaded environments, including transactional memory and obstruction-free software transactional memory.

Healthcare Applications

Research and project involvement in healthcare-related computing, including work associated with Media Lab Asia, Government of India.

AI & Image Analysis

Recent research includes AI/deep-learning approaches for image-based classification and analysis, including research on cancer-cell texture identification and tea-leaf pathology and pest classification.
